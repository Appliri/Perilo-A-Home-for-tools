02

your garden

Tap, drag and stretch what grows.

Whistle grows tall plants · bass grows mushrooms · highs grow flowers · rhythm makes them dance.

Tap any organism to hear its note. Drag a plant's tip to stretch and bend it (taller = higher, bent = wobblier). Drag a mushroom sideways to widen it (wider = deeper). Drag a creature up or down to stretch it. Scroll over one to resize.

tapping empty ground plants

Tap a planet to hear it · drag it to move its orbit · drag an orbit ring to stretch it into an ellipse (stretched orbits swing the rhythm) · scroll over a planet to resize. Size sets pitch, distance sets echo, collisions make chords.

selected planet

Drag across empty space to string a new note — shorter strings sing higher, and the note name shows as you draw. Sweep across strings to strum (with a mouse, just glide over them). Double-click a string to remove it. Turn on the music box to let the harp play itself.
