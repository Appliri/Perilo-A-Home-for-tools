Perilo · 3D room

Scribble Den

Draw in the air, dress every stroke in a material, then put your sculpture on a lit stage. Each tile of the cube holds one piece — its six faces are Perilo's six landscapes.

    1. Gallery. Drag the cube to browse. Click a sculpture to view it, or an empty tile to start one there.
    2. Studio. Hold and drag to scribble tubes, beads and ribbons in 3D. Alt-click or double-click any stroke to restyle it without leaving Draw.
    3. Stage. Light it, pick a backdrop, animate strokes and export a live wallpaper.

    Keyboard: focus the canvas, then ←→↑↓ rotate and +− zoom. Projects stay in this browser until you clear its data.
